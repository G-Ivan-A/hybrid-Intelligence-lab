# BA review after the machine gate

- Confirm each evidence anchor was actually retrieved and may be used.
- Confirm the product binding and system boundary match the request.
- Review FR, UC, NFR, backward compatibility and client Release meaning.
- Resolve every open question and approve or reject publication explicitly.

The runner's `contract_mode` event records this obligation. It is never a
machine PASS or a record of an approval that has not occurred.
